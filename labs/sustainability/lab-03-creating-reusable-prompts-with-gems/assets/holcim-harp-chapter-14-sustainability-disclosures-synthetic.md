# HARP Chapter 14 — Non-Financial and Sustainability Disclosures

**Holcim Accounting and Reporting Principles (HARP) — Synthetic Training Excerpt**

## 14.1 Purpose and Scope

This chapter sets out how Group entities prepare non-financial and sustainability disclosures for inclusion in the Annual Report and other external reporting. It applies to all consolidated plants and business units reporting under operational control.

## 14.2 Consolidation Boundary

14.2.1 Sustainability KPIs are consolidated using the **operational control approach**: a facility is in scope if Holcim directs its operating policies, regardless of ownership percentage.

14.2.2 Joint operations where Holcim holds operational control are reported at 100%. Joint operations without operational control are excluded from Scope 1 and Scope 2 KPIs but disclosed in a separate equity-share note.

## 14.3 GHG Emissions Reporting Requirements

14.3.1 Scope 1 and Scope 2 emissions are mandatory for every consolidated plant, every reporting quarter, using the calculation methods maintained by the Sustainable Development (SD) function.

14.3.2 Scope 3 emissions are reported annually at Group level. Plant-level Scope 3 estimates are optional supporting detail and must be labeled "estimate" if included.

14.3.3 Any plant with missing or partial Scope 1 or Scope 2 data at quarter-close must be flagged to SD and Group Reporting before the disclosure is finalized. Do not submit an estimate in place of a missing figure without an approved estimation method.

## 14.4 Materiality Thresholds

14.4.1 A sustainability KPI is presumptively material at Group level if it could reasonably influence an investor's assessment of climate transition risk, consistent with the Group's CSRD double-materiality assessment.

14.4.2 Plant-level KPIs below 0.5% of Group production volume may be aggregated into a regional total rather than disclosed individually, unless a controversy or incident makes the plant individually material.

## 14.5 Transition Plans and Targets

14.5.1 Any plant-level transition plan referenced in external disclosures must be approved by the plant's regional Sustainability lead and logged in the Group transition plan register before publication.

14.5.2 Site-level targets disclosed externally must trace to an approved Group or regional target; a plant may not publish a standalone target that has not been reviewed by SD.

## 14.6 Sign-off and Assurance

14.6.1 All sustainability disclosures included in the Annual Report require sign-off from the plant Sustainability contact, the regional SD lead, and Group Reporting before submission to external assurance.

14.6.2 Limited assurance procedures apply to Scope 1, Scope 2, and headline Group KPIs (thermal substitution rate, renewable electricity share, water withdrawal intensity). Reasonable assurance is not required unless otherwise instructed by Group Reporting.

## 14.7 Data Retention and Audit Trail

14.7.1 Source data supporting any disclosed KPI must be retained for a minimum of 7 years and traceable to the originating plant system.

14.7.2 Restatements of a prior-year disclosed KPI require a documented reason and must be flagged in the current-year disclosure as a restatement, not silently corrected.
