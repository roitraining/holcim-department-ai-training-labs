# Holcim Geocycle Waste Acceptance Notes (Synthetic Training Content)

> Synthetic training material for Holcim Operational Excellence labs. Not a real customer contract or plant permit.

## What Geocycle does (training summary)

Geocycle supports Holcim kiln operations by preparing suitable waste streams as **alternative fuels (AF)** and alternative raw materials. Higher safe thermal substitution can support Operational Excellence goals alongside CEM clinker-factor reduction.

## Intake checklist (synthetic)

Before accepting a new waste stream for evaluation, capture:

1. Waste type and physical form (solid, liquid, sludge)
2. Estimated monthly tonnage and delivery window
3. Known contaminants or restricted substances (yes/no; list if yes)
4. Customer contact and site of origin
5. Whether a trial burn or lab characterization is already available

## Example acceptable response pattern

**Question:** Can we accept spent solvents next month?

**Grounded answer style:** Confirm that spent solvents require characterization for calorific value, chlorine, and heavy metals before a trial. Do not promise acceptance dates or volumes unless the user supplies an approved plant decision.

## Phrases to avoid

- Guarantees of acceptance without characterization
- Invented permit numbers, emission limits, or pricing
- Claims that AF substitution automatically meets a specific CEM product carbon claim without evidence
