These three files are the **Knowledge** attachments for the `Holcim Year-End Reporting Assistant` Gem built in Task 2:

- `holcim-harp-chapter-14-sustainability-disclosures-synthetic.md` — a fictional HARP (Holcim Accounting and Reporting Principles) chapter on non-financial and sustainability disclosures.
- `holcim-sd-definitions-glossary-synthetic.md` — a fictional Sustainable Development (SD) function glossary defining the emissions and reporting terms used across this course.
- `holcim-annual-report-fy2024-sustainability-excerpt-synthetic.md` — a fictional excerpt from a prior-year Annual Report sustainability chapter, with invented Group KPIs and a restatement note.

All three are **synthetic training content only**. Clause numbers, definitions, figures, and quotes are invented for this course and do not represent Holcim's real HARP manual, SD glossary, or published Annual Report. Do not treat them as real policy or real disclosed figures, and do not reuse them outside the classroom.

## Why this design

This lab was shaped around a real customer idea: a chatbot/assistant that takes HARP chapters, SD definitions, and annual reports so it can answer year-end reporting clarification questions automatically. Gemini Gems support exactly this through their **Knowledge** feature (up to 10 attached files that stay with the Gem), so Task 2 builds a working, scaled-down version of that assistant using synthetic stand-ins for the three source types.
