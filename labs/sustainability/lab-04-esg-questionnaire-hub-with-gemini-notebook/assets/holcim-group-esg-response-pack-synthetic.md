# Holcim Group ESG Response Pack

**Synthetic Training Excerpt — pre-approved facts for customer ESG and sustainability questionnaires**

This pack collects Group-level facts the Sustainability team can reuse when answering a customer's ESG questionnaire. Answers are organized under the four themes used by common third-party ratings (for example EcoVadis): Environment, Labor and Human Rights, Ethics, and Sustainable Procurement.

## Environment

- **Environmental Management System:** 78% of Integrated Plants and 64% of Grinding Stations hold ISO 14001 certification Group-wide. Remaining sites are on a certification roadmap through FY2027.
- **GHG reduction target:** Group Specific Net CO2 target of 475 kg per tonne cementitious by 2030, down from 542 kg per tonne in FY2024 (see Annual Report FY2024 excerpt).
- **Renewable electricity target:** Group target of 60% renewable electricity share by 2030, up from 33% in FY2024.
- **Water stewardship:** Group Water Withdrawal Intensity target of 0.22 m3 per tonne by 2030. Regional water stewardship plans are logged in the Group transition plan register per HARP Chapter 14.5.1.
- **Waste and circularity:** Alternative Fuels and Raw Materials (AFR) program co-processes waste-derived materials as kiln fuel substitutes at all Integrated Plants; Group average thermal substitution rate was 35% in FY2024.
- **Low-carbon product range:** ECOPact lower-carbon concrete range, minimum 30% embodied carbon reduction versus standard concrete, available in more than 30 markets.

## Labor and Human Rights

- **Health and safety management system:** 82% of plants Group-wide hold ISO 45001 certification.
- **Human Rights Policy:** Group Human Rights Policy (fictional reference: HR-POL-02, approved 2023) covers non-discrimination, prohibition of forced and child labor, and freedom of association across owned operations.
- **Training:** Average of 18 hours of job-related training per employee per year, Group-wide (fictional figure).
- **Diversity target:** Group target of 30% women in leadership roles by 2028 (fictional figure, baseline 21% in FY2024).
- **Grievance mechanism:** Employees can raise labor and human rights concerns through the Group Speak Up line (see Ethics section) without retaliation.

## Ethics

- **Code of Business Conduct:** All employees complete Code of Business Conduct training within 90 days of joining and annually thereafter (fictional policy).
- **Anti-corruption:** Group Anti-Corruption Policy (fictional reference: ETH-POL-01) prohibits bribery and facilitation payments and requires due diligence on third-party intermediaries in higher-risk markets.
- **Whistleblower mechanism:** The Group Speak Up line is available in local languages, operated by an independent third party, and open to employees, suppliers, and customers.
- **Competition law:** Group Competition Law training completion rate of 94% among commercial and procurement staff (fictional figure).

## Sustainable Procurement

- **Supplier Code of Conduct:** All direct-material suppliers are required to accept the Group Supplier Code of Conduct, covering environmental, labor, and ethics expectations.
- **Supplier ESG screening:** 61% of direct-material spend is covered by supplier ESG screening Group-wide, with a target of 85% by 2027 (fictional figures).
- **Responsible sourcing:** Raw material sourcing programs prioritize suppliers with credible environmental management and, where relevant, quarry rehabilitation and biodiversity commitments.

## How to use this pack

- Prefer a figure from this pack or from the Annual Report excerpt over an invented number.
- If a customer question is more specific than this pack (for example, a single plant's certification status), say so rather than generalizing the Group figure as plant-specific.
- If nothing here or in the other sources answers the question, say the answer needs input from the SD team rather than guessing.
