# Sustainable Development (SD) Definitions Glossary

**Holcim Sustainable Development function — Synthetic Training Excerpt**

This glossary gives the Group's approved definitions for terms used in sustainability disclosures. When a term appears in a HARP chapter or an Annual Report, use the definition below rather than an external or informal definition.

## Emissions terms

**Scope 1 Emissions** — Direct GHG emissions from sources owned or controlled by Holcim, primarily fuel combustion in kilns and on-site vehicles.

**Scope 2 Emissions** — Indirect GHG emissions from purchased electricity, heat, and steam consumed at Holcim facilities. Reported using the location-based method unless a market-based figure is separately labeled.

**Scope 3 Emissions** — All other indirect emissions in the value chain, including purchased raw materials, transport, and use of sold products. Reported at Group level annually per HARP 14.3.2.

**Gross CO2 Emissions** — Total Scope 1 CO2 emissions from a plant before any offset or credit is applied, expressed in tonnes.

**Specific Net CO2** — Gross CO2 emissions per tonne of cementitious material produced, net of alternative raw material substitution, expressed in kilograms per tonne. This is the Group's headline decarbonization KPI.

## Fuel and energy terms

**Thermal Substitution Rate (TSR)** — The share of kiln thermal energy supplied by alternative fuels (for example waste-derived fuels) instead of fossil fuels, expressed as a percentage. Applies only to Integrated Plants; Grinding Stations do not operate a kiln and report TSR as not applicable.

**Alternative Fuels and Raw Materials (AFR)** — Waste-derived materials co-processed in the kiln as a fuel or raw material substitute, reducing both fossil fuel use and landfill waste.

**Renewable Electricity Share** — The percentage of a plant's purchased and self-generated electricity that comes from renewable sources, per the Group's renewable electricity accounting method.

## Plant classification terms

**Integrated Plant** — A cement plant that operates a kiln and produces clinker on site, in addition to grinding. Integrated Plants report Scope 1 kiln emissions and TSR.

**Grinding Station** — A facility that grinds clinker and other materials into finished cement without operating a kiln. Grinding Stations have materially lower direct Scope 1 emissions than Integrated Plants and do not report TSR.

## Water and reporting process terms

**Water Withdrawal Intensity** — Total water withdrawn per tonne of cementitious material produced, expressed in cubic meters per tonne.

**Transition Plan** — A plant- or Group-level document describing the pathway and interim milestones toward a published decarbonization target. Must be logged in the Group transition plan register before being referenced externally, per HARP 14.5.1.

**Restatement** — A correction to a previously disclosed KPI value. Must be flagged as a restatement with a documented reason, never silently corrected, per HARP 14.7.2.

**Materiality (Sustainability KPI)** — A KPI is material if it could reasonably influence an investor's assessment of climate transition risk, consistent with the Group's CSRD double-materiality assessment. See HARP 14.4.1 for thresholds.
