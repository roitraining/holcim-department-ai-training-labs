# Lab assets

Supporting files for **Prompt Engineering Mastery with Gemini for Holcim Sustainability Teams**.

- `holcim-logo.png` / `holcim-logo.svg` — Holcim wordmark sourced from Wikimedia Commons (originally from holcim.com)

Use these only for this internal training exercise (ECOPact infographic practice).

## Synthetic plant data

The six-plant CSRD readiness table used in Task 2 is embedded directly in `lab.md` (learners copy-paste it into Gemini). All plant IDs, figures, and ratings are **synthetic training data** invented for this lab and do not represent real Holcim emissions, targets, or plans.

## ECOPact facts

The approved ECOPact facts referenced in Task 1 and Task 3 prompts (minimum 30% embodied carbon reduction, availability in 30+ markets, LEED and BREEAM contribution, ECOPact+ near-zero variant) reflect Holcim's public product messaging at time of writing. Verify current figures against holcim.com before reusing this content outside the classroom.
