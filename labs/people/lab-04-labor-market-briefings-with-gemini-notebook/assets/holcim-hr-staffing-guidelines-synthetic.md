# Holcim People — Synthetic HR Staffing Guidelines (Training Only)

Document owner: Holcim People Excellence (synthetic)
Version: Lab training v1.0
Audience: People Business Partners and plant People leaders

## 1. Purpose

These guidelines explain how Holcim People teams plan hiring, contractor mix, and internal mobility for Building Materials and Building Solutions sites. Use them to prepare leadership discussions on labor availability. This document is synthetic training content and does not replace real Holcim policy.

## 2. Holcim regions in scope

For People planning discussions, use these management regions:

- Europe (EU)
- Latin America (LATAM) — includes South America priority markets in this lab scenario
- Asia, Middle East and Africa (AMEA)

When leaders say “EMEA,” map the conversation to Europe plus relevant AMEA countries unless they specify otherwise.

## 3. Workforce planning principles

1. Health and safety first: never staff a critical plant role below the safety minimum for the shift pattern.
2. Prefer internal mobility before external hiring for leadership and scarce technical roles.
3. Keep a balanced mix of Operational and Non-Operational headcount aligned to production plans.
4. Treat Full-Time Equivalent (FTE) as the unit of planning; convert contractors to FTE where used.
5. Document assumptions for attrition, time-to-fill, and training runway before requesting headcount.

## 5. Time-to-fill standards (synthetic targets)

- Frontline Operational roles: 30–45 days
- Technical specialists (process, maintenance, quality): 60–90 days
- Plant leadership (SM/MM): 90–120 days
- Scarce digital / automation skills: 120+ days (escalate early)

If a requisition exceeds the target by 25%, the People Business Partner must present mitigation options: overtime, temporary contractors, internal secondments, or schedule redesign.

## 6. Contingent labor rules (synthetic)

- Contractors may cover surge production and short-term projects, not permanent leadership accountability.
- Contractor FTE should stay below 18% of site FTE unless the Regional People Director approves an exception.
- Every contractor assignment needs an exit or conversion plan dated within 12 months.

## 7. Labor availability escalation triggers

Escalate to regional leadership when any two of the following are true for 60 consecutive days:

- Vacancy rate for critical roles above 8%
- Overtime hours above 12% of total worked hours
- Safety-critical shifts staffed with more than 20% temporary coverage
- Offer-accept rate below 70% for technical roles

## 8. Meeting briefing requirements

Before a management meeting on labor availability, People partners should prepare:

1. Current vacancy and FTE snapshot by region and role family
2. External labor market signals for the same geographies
3. Three mitigation options with cost/time tradeoffs
4. Risks to safety, production, and customer commitments
5. Clear ask (approve hiring surge, approve contractor exception, delay a project, or invest in training)

## 9. Data handling

Use anonymized workforce extracts for training and classroom demos. Do not paste confidential employee identifiers, medical data, or performance ratings into public AI tools.
