# Holcim Annual Report FY2024 — Sustainability Chapter (Excerpt)

**Synthetic Training Excerpt**

## Message from Sustainable Development

"FY2024 was a year of measured progress. Group Specific Net CO2 declined to 542 kg per tonne of cementitious material, a reduction of 3.1% versus FY2023, driven by higher alternative fuel use and continued ramp-up of ECOPact volumes. We are not yet where we need to be on Scope 3, and we said so plainly to our Board this year."

## Restatement Note

The FY2023 Renewable Electricity Share figure was restated from 26% to 28% following a correction to metering data at two European plants, disclosed in accordance with HARP Chapter 14.7.2. No other prior-year figures were restated.

## Assurance Statement (Summary)

Scope 1 and Scope 2 emissions, thermal substitution rate, renewable electricity share, and water withdrawal intensity received limited assurance from the Group's external assurance provider for FY2024. Scope 3 emissions were not subject to assurance this cycle.

## Regional Highlights

**Europe:** Specific Net CO2 improved fastest in Europe, supported by higher grinding-station mix and continued clinker substitution.

**Latin America:** Thermal substitution rate grew the most in Latin America as co-processing agreements expanded in Brazil and Mexico.

**Asia, Middle East and Africa:** Water withdrawal intensity remains highest in this region; a regional water stewardship plan is in development and will be logged in the Group transition plan register during FY2025 per HARP Chapter 14.5.1.

## Looking Ahead to FY2025

Sustainable Development will prioritize closing Scope 3 estimation gaps, expanding ECOPact+ availability, and preparing the Group's first full-year disclosure under the EU Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive (CSRD) and ESRS E1.
