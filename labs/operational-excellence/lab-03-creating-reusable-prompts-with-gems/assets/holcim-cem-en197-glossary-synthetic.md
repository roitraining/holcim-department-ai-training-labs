# Holcim CEM Types Glossary (Synthetic Training Content)

> Synthetic training material for Holcim Operational Excellence labs. Not an official Holcim policy document.

## Purpose

Quick reference for EN 197-1 cement families used in Operational Excellence discussions about clinker factor and low-carbon product mix.

## Cement families (EN 197-1)

| Type | Plain-language meaning | Typical clinker intensity |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| CEM I | Ordinary Portland Cement; mostly clinker | Highest |
| CEM II | Portland-composite cement with moderate SCM addition | Medium-high |
| CEM III | Blast-furnace cement with substantial slag | Medium-low |
| CEM IV | Pozzolanic cement | Lower |
| CEM V | Composite cement with multiple SCMs | Often lowest among common blends |

## Key OE terms

- **Clinker factor:** Share of clinker in cementitious product. Lower is generally better for embodied carbon when performance requirements are still met.
- **SCM (supplementary cementitious material):** Materials such as slag, fly ash, calcined clay, or recycled concrete fines that replace part of the clinker.
- **ECOPlanet (training shorthand):** Holcim low-carbon cement positioning used in this course. Always treat performance claims as user-supplied or Knowledge-grounded; never invent market statistics.
