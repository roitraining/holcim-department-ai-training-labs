# Holcim OE Geocycle Alternative Fuels Brief (Synthetic)

> Synthetic training content for Gemini Notebook labs. Not operational guidance for a real plant.

## Why AF matters for OE

Geocycle thermal substitution reduces reliance on fossil fuels in the kiln. In OE reviews, AF progress is discussed next to CEM clinker-factor progress because both move the low-carbon agenda—but they are not the same lever.

## Illustrative signals (training set)

- Thermal Substitution Rate (%) varies widely by region in the synthetic SiteData file.
- Sites marked **OE Priority Site = Yes** often combine high clinker factor with low AF, or the reverse imbalance.
- Waste tonnes processed are correlated with, but not identical to, thermal substitution percentage.

## Safe briefing language

- Prefer “supports kiln fuel substitution” over absolute carbon claims.
- Never invent customer waste-contract volumes.
- If asked for a regulatory limit not in the sources, say the Notebook does not contain that figure.
