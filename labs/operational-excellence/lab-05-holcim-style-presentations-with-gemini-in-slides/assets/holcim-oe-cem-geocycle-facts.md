# Holcim OE source file: CEM and Geocycle

Use this file as the only source of figures for the Operational Excellence leadership deck. If a number is not written here, leave it out. Do not invent plant results, country rankings, or customer volumes.

These figures are published Holcim results and published targets. They are not a local plant report.

## What this update is about

Holcim describes itself as the leading partner for sustainable construction. In 2025, net sales were CHF 15.7 billion. Premium brands named on [holcim.com](https://www.holcim.com/) include ECOPact, ECOPlanet, ECOCycle, and Ytong.

This deck covers two different levers:

- **CEM formulations.** Lower the clinker factor, and grow ECOPlanet.
- **Geocycle energy.** Raise the thermal substitution rate by using alternative fuels in the kiln.

Do not present those levers as one metric. Clinker factor is the share of clinker in cement. Thermal substitution rate is the share of kiln heat that comes from alternative fuels.

## CEM: clinker factor and ECOPlanet

Clinker is the intermediate material made mainly from limestone and clay at about 1,450°C. The clinker factor is the average percentage of clinker in cement.

| Measure | 2023 | 2024 | 2025 | Published 2030 target |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Clinker factor | 71% | 70% | 70% | 65% |

The clinker factor was stable in 2025. The 2030 target is 65%, with a further reduction by 2050. Holcim lowers the factor by replacing clinker with mineral components:

- Recycled cement paste from construction demolition materials
- Calcined clay, pozzolana, and reclaimed ashes
- Slag and fly ash
- Limestone and gypsum

Calcined clay can lower CO2 by up to 50% compared with standard cement production. In 2025, five sites produced calcined clay. The published aim is at least 30 sites by 2030.

ECOPlanet is Holcim's low-carbon cement brand. Holcim states that ECOPlanet delivers at least 30% lower CO2 than ordinary CEM I / OPC cement, without offsets.

| Measure | 2024 | 2025 | Published 2030 target |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ECOPlanet share of cement net sales | 34% | 36% | More than 50% |

ECOPact is the low-carbon ready-mix concrete brand, not a cement brand. In 2025 it was 31% of ready-mix net sales, up from 26% in 2024. Do not describe ECOPact as ECOPlanet.

Formulation improvements, including clinker substitution, are expected to contribute about 40% to 50% of the Scope 1 reductions Holcim needs by 2030.

## Geocycle: thermal substitution

Geocycle turns non-recyclable waste and byproducts into alternative fuels and raw materials for cement kilns. That includes biomass where it is available. This is the energy lever, not the formulations lever.

| Measure | 2023 | 2024 | 2025 | Published 2030 target |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Thermal substitution rate (alternative fuels plus biomass) | 35% | 38% | 39% | 50% |
| Thermal substitution rate from biomass | 14% | 15% | 17% | Not published in this file |

In 2025, alternative fuels accounted for 39% of Holcim's thermal energy demand for clinker production. Geocycle recycled 12.6 million tons of waste and byproducts for use as energy or raw materials. In Europe, 14 cement plants already used alternative fuels for more than 80% of their thermal energy.

## Why the two levers sit in one update

Net Scope 1 emissions were 502 kg of CO2 per ton of cementitious materials in 2025, down from 515 kg in 2024, and 11% below the 2020 baseline. The published 2030 target is below 400 kg.

Holcim's three Scope 1 levers are formulations, energy, and advanced technologies such as carbon capture. This deck stops at the first two:

- Formulations: clinker factor and ECOPlanet
- Energy: Geocycle and the thermal substitution rate

Carbon capture is out of scope for these six slides.
